WHY WE PROCESS YOUR DATA (LEGAL BASIS AND PURPOSES)
-----------------------------------------------------
Your data is processed on the basis of your explicit consent and for the
following specific purposes:

  1. Program Administration
     Processing is necessary to manage your membership, track earned points,
     and administer reward redemptions.

  2. Service Communications
     We send transactional messages (e.g., point updates, account alerts)
     necessary for the operation of your Wallet account.

  3. Personalised Offers and Campaigns
     With your consent, we process your purchase history and preferences to
     deliver exclusive offers, loyalty rewards, and personalised promotions.

  4. Legal Compliance
     We process data as required to comply with Albanian law, including
     Law No. 9887 dated 10.03.2008 "On the Protection of Personal Data".

----------------------------
Your data is retained for the duration of your active membership. Once your

If we become aware that personal data has been collected from a person under

immediately.
